Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

MELTOS is a personal performance platform. To work properly, it needs to understand your goals, habits and progress. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Who We Are

MELTOS ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Luhv+ and accessible at meltos.netlify.app. For questions about this policy, contact us at meltosbyluhv@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account data — your name and email address when you register
  • Goals & habits — the goals, daily tasks and habits you set inside the app
  • Progress data — your habit completions, goal progress percentages and streaks
  • Quiz responses — your answers to the onboarding quiz (peak hour, obstacles, 90-day goal)
  • Energy logs — your daily energy check-ins (1–5 scale)
  • Coach conversations — messages you send to and receive from the AI Coach
  • Journal entries — any notes or reflections you choose to write
  • Focus sessions — timer sessions and linked goals

We do not collect payment card details (processed directly by Square), precise location, or any data from your device beyond what is necessary to run the app.

3. Why We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used exclusively to provide and improve the MELTOS service:

  • To power the AI Coach — your goals, habits and progress are sent to the coach so it can give you relevant, personalised guidance
  • To calculate your Performance Score and weekly reviews
  • To generate your Roadmap and daily task suggestions
  • To improve the quality of coaching responses and the Luhv+ knowledge base (in anonymised, aggregated form only)
  • To send you product updates if you opt in

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

4. The AI Coach & Your Data

When you interact with the AI Coach, your messages — along with relevant context from your goals, habits and energy — are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. This is necessary for the coach to give you personalised, actionable advice.

Anthropic processes this data under their own Privacy Policy. We do not share your name or email with Anthropic — only the conversational and performance context needed to generate a response.

By using the AI Coach, you consent to this processing.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase. All connections are encrypted via TLS. We use industry-standard security practices including hashed passwords and JWT-authenticated API access.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by emailing meltosbyluhv@gmail.com.

6. Sharing Your Data

We share your data only with the services necessary to run MELTOS:

  • Anthropic — AI Coach responses (conversational context only)
  • Square — Payment processing (we share only your email to match your account after payment)
  • Supabase — Database hosting
  • Render — Server hosting
  • Netlify — Frontend hosting

We do not share your data with any marketing platforms, data brokers, or third parties beyond what is listed above.

7. Community Ranking

If you choose to join the Community Ranking feature, your streak count will be visible to other MELTOS users alongside a nickname you choose. Your real name and email are never displayed. Participation is optional and you can opt out at any time from within the app.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data (available via the report download feature in the app)

To exercise any of these rights, email meltosbyluhv@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

MELTOS is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or via a notice in the app. Continued use of MELTOS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at meltosbyluhv@gmail.com.

Install on your device

MELTOS is a Progressive Web App — no app store needed.
Install it in seconds directly from your browser.

🍎
iPhone / iPad
  1. 1 Open meltosbyluhv.netlify.app in Safari
  2. 2 Tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom
  3. 3 Tap "Add to Home Screen" and confirm
  4. 4 Open MELTOS from your home screen like any app 🏆
🤖
Android
  1. 1 Open meltosbyluhv.netlify.app in Chrome
  2. 2 Tap the ⋮ menu (top right)
  3. 3 Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
  4. 4 MELTOS installs as a native app on your Android 🏆
💻
